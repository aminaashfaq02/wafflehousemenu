Waffle House Menu Prices: What a Meal Costs in 2026

Expect to pay about $9.90 for a two egg breakfast with coffee and about $15.00 for the All-Star Special with coffee. Lunch with a Texas melt runs about $11.95, and a T-bone dinner with a drink about $21.50. Tax and tip are extra, and prices vary by location.

Typical prices compiled by Waffle Guide on November 4, 2026. Prices vary by location.

The full menu tells you what each item costs. This guide answers the question most people have: how much will I spend? Using typical 2026 prices, here is what a breakfast, lunch, dinner or late-night stop usually comes to, what a family of four pays, and where the best value is.

The All-Star Special is the best-value full breakfast: ordered separately, the same eggs, bacon, toast, side and waffle cost about $15.05, against about $12.35 for the combo. More in our All-Star Special guide.

What a family of four pays

Two adults ordering the All-Star Special (one with bacon, one with sausage), two children sharing two egg breakfasts, two coffees and two milks come to about $49.80 before tax and tip. Swapping the All-Stars for two egg breakfasts would bring it down to about $39.60.

Waffle House does not list a separate kids’ menu on its nutrition pages, so this example uses regular menu items.

Tax, tips and why your bill differs

  • Menu prices do not include sales tax, which is added at the register and differs by state and city.
  • Waffle House is a table-service restaurant, so leaving a tip for your server is customary in the US.
  • Restaurants near highways, airports and in bigger cities often charge a little more than the typical prices shown here.
  • Toppings, extra eggs, cheese and upgraded waffles all add to the bill. Each hashbrown topping usually adds about $1.00.
