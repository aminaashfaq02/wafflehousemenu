Waffle House Texas Melts Compared: Which One Should You Order?

The Texas melts are the lunch and dinner side of Waffle House: thick grilled Texas toast, two slices of melted American cheese, grilled onions and a hot filling. There are five to choose from, and they differ more than you might expect in protein, calories and price. Here they are side by side.

WG
By Editorial Team
Waffle Guide

The short answer

For the most protein, order the Texas Grilled Chicken Melt (41g of protein, 660 calories). For the fewest calories, the Texas Sausage Melt (645). Most melts typically cost about $5.95–$6.50; the bacon cheesesteak is the priciest at about $10.35.

The five Texas melts

Texas Cheesesteak Melt

Thin-sliced chuck cheesesteak with grilled onions and two slices of American cheese on Texas toast.

  • About 650 calories, 28g of protein, 42g of carbs and 1,400mg of sodium.
  • Typically about $5.95 ($5.40–$6.50 seen).

Texas Bacon Cheesesteak Melt

The Texas Cheesesteak Melt with bacon added: cheesesteak, grilled onions, bacon and two slices of American cheese on Texas toast.

  • About 740 calories, 33g of protein, 42g of carbs and 1,750mg of sodium.
  • Typically about $10.35 ($9.95–$10.80 seen).

Texas Angus Patty Melt

A quarter-pound Angus patty with grilled onions and two slices of American cheese on Texas toast.

  • About 730 calories, 26g of protein, 42g of carbs and 1,160mg of sodium.
  • Typically about $6.50 ($5.45–$9.45 seen).

Texas Grilled Chicken Melt

Grilled chicken with grilled onions and two slices of American cheese on Texas toast.

  • About 660 calories, 41g of protein, 43g of carbs and 2,040mg of sodium.
  • Typically about $5.95 ($5.45–$6.50 seen).

Texas Sausage Melt

A sausage patty with grilled onions and two slices of American cheese on Texas toast.

  • About 645 calories, 16g of protein, 42g of carbs and 1,360mg of sodium.
  • Typically about $5.95 ($5.45–$6.50 seen).

Add-ons and what they change

Melts take the same toppings as hashbrowns and bowls. Add-ons usually cost extra; here is what they add nutritionally:

Add-onCaloriesProteinSodium
Jalapeños+10+0g+390mg
Grilled mushrooms+20+2g+280mg
Grilled tomatoes+5+0g+0mg
Extra slice of cheese+50+3g+250mg
Bacon+90+5g+350mg
Bert’s Chili+80+5g+380mg

Jalapeños and grilled tomatoes add flavour for almost no calories, though jalapeños bring a surprising amount of sodium.

Make it a meal

A melt on its own is a sandwich. Most people add hashbrowns: a regular order adds about 190 calories and typically $3.45, so a cheesesteak melt with hashbrowns comes to about 840 calories and $9.40.

If you want the melt filling without the toast, the cheesesteak and chicken melt hashbrown bowls put it on a large order of hashbrowns instead (625 and 635 calories).

The breakfast melts

At breakfast, two Texas melts swap the grilled filling for eggs and breakfast meat:
