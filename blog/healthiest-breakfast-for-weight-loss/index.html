The Healthiest Breakfast at Waffle House for Weight Loss

Waffle House is not a diet restaurant, but almost everything is cooked to order, which makes it easy to build a breakfast that fits a calorie target. The trick is knowing where the calories are. Here are the lighter plates that still fill you up, the swaps that save the most, and the orders that add up faster than you would think.

WG
By Editorial Team
Waffle Guide

The short answer

For the fewest calories with real staying power, order two scrambled eggs with sliced tomatoes and wheat toast (about 410 calories and 22g of protein). For more protein, grilled chicken and two eggs with sliced tomatoes comes to about 330 calories and 43g of protein, and the Chicken Fiesta Protein Bowl is about 540 calories with 52g.

Four lighter breakfasts that still fill you up

Each of these is built from regular menu items, so any Waffle House can make it. Calories and protein are for the plate exactly as listed.

PlateCaloriesProteinCarbs
Two scrambled eggs, sliced tomatoes and wheat toast41022g25g
Grilled chicken, two scrambled eggs and sliced tomatoes33043g4g
Two scrambled eggs, city ham and grits38029g24g
Classic waffle with a side of city ham52023g62g

If you want something already on the menu, the Chicken Fiesta Protein Bowl (grilled chicken, three scrambled eggs, cheese, onions, tomatoes and jalapeños) is about 540 calories with 52g of protein and only 12g of carbs.

The swaps that save the most

Most of the calories in a Waffle House breakfast come from the bread, the side and the meat. These changes make the biggest difference:

SwapCalories saved
Leave off the toast230
Sliced tomatoes instead of hashbrowns180
City ham instead of sausage150
Bacon instead of sausage120
Grits instead of hashbrowns100
Wheat toast instead of raisin toast80
Hold the cheese (per slice)50

Wheat toast also has twice the protein of white toast (8g against 4g) for 10 fewer calories. Hashbrowns can be ordered cooked dry, without the usual oil, if you ask; the figures here are for a standard order.

How to order a lighter All-Star Special

The All-Star Special does not have to be the heaviest thing on the table. Choose city ham, sliced tomatoes and wheat toast and it comes to about 930 calories, compared with 1,270 for the version with sausage, hashbrowns and white toast. That is 340 calories less for the same price.

The waffle on its own is 410 calories before butter and syrup, so sharing it or taking half home is the next easiest cut. More in our All-Star Special guide.

Get more protein for your calories

Protein helps a breakfast keep you full. Per 100 calories, these are the most efficient choices:

Country ham is high in protein but also very salty, at about 1,720mg of sodium a slice. See every dish with 20g of protein or more on the high-protein menu.

None of these are off limits, but it helps to know what you are ordering:

  • Drinks count too: lemonade is about 210 calories and sweet tea 70, while unsweet tea, Diet Coke and black coffee are close to zero.
  • Toppings add up: sausage gravy adds 90 calories to hashbrowns and chili 80, while onions (15) and tomatoes (5) barely register.
