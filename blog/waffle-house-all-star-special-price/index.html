Quick Answer: Waffle House All Star Special Price & Snapshot (2026)

In 2026, the Waffle House All Star Special price ranges between $12.35 and $13.55 at most suburban and highway locations (with metro areas reaching up to $14.25). The meal includes 5 full plates: two fresh eggs cooked any style, your choice of breakfast meat, regular hashbrowns or Southern grits, a golden sweet cream waffle, and grilled toast or biscuit. Bundling these items in the All-Star combo saves customers $4.00 to $5.50+ compared to ordering each component a la carte.

What Comes With the Waffle House All-Star Special?

5 Pro Tips & Secret Ordering Hacks for the All-Star Special

Experienced Waffle House regulars know that the printed diner menu is just a starting suggestion. Use these five insider diner hacks to elevate your All-Star Special experience:

#1

Upgrade to Texas Toast for Free

Instead of standard white or wheat toast, request grilled Texas toast. Most locations substitute the thick-cut, buttered Texas bread at zero extra charge.

#2

Ask for "Crispy" or "Well-Done" Spuds

If you dislike soggy potatoes, ask the server to call your hashbrowns "scattered crispy" or "scattered well-done". The grill op leaves them on the heat longer for an ultra-crunchy crust.

#3

Order Eggs "Basted"

Basted eggs are cooked on the griddle under a metal dome with a splash of water or ice chips. The steam cooks the egg whites gently without needing extra butter oil.

#4

Waffle Dessert Timing

Do not want your sweet cream waffle getting cold while eating eggs and bacon? Ask your server to "drop the waffle after breakfast" so it arrives piping hot for dessert.
