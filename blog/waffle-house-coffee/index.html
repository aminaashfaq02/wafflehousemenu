What Coffee Does Waffle House Use? The Brand, the Blends and the Price

Waffle House coffee is roasted by Royal Cup Coffee & Tea of Birmingham, Alabama, which has supplied the chain since the 1970s. It is made from 100% Arabica beans grown in Central and South America and comes in three versions: Classic Blend, Specialty Dark Roast and Premium Decaf. A cup typically costs about $2.65.

Typical prices compiled by Waffle Guide on November 4, 2026. Prices vary by location.

Coffee is the quiet constant at Waffle House: it is on the table at breakfast, at lunch and at three in the morning. If you have ever wondered who makes it, which blends there are and what a cup costs, here is everything worth knowing.

Who makes Waffle House coffee

Waffle House coffee comes from Royal Cup Coffee & Tea, a family-owned roaster founded in 1896 and based in Birmingham, Alabama. The two companies have worked together since the 1970s, and Royal Cup roasts the beans before they are shipped to restaurants and brewed on site.

The beans are 100% Arabica, grown on farms in Central and South America. Arabica is the species most specialty coffee is made from, generally smoother and less bitter than the Robusta used in many cheaper blends.

The three blends

BlendWhat to expect
Classic BlendThe standard cup most restaurants pour: a medium roast meant to go with everything from waffles to steak.
Specialty Dark RoastA darker roast with a stronger, roastier flavour for people who like their coffee bold.
Premium DecafDecaffeinated coffee for late-night visits.

Ask your server which blends are brewing. Hot tea is also on the menu if you would rather skip the coffee.

How much a cup costs

A cup of coffee typically costs about $2.65, and published 2026 price lists show it from $2.45 to $2.95 depending on the restaurant. Waffle House does not publish a refill policy, so ask your server how refills work at your restaurant.

Calories and caffeine

A cup of black coffee has about 5 calories. Cream, half-and-half and sugar add more, depending on how much you use. Waffle House does not publish the caffeine content of its coffee, but as a general guide, an 8-ounce cup of brewed coffee has around 95 milligrams of caffeine, and decaf has little.

If you are watching calories, black coffee and unsweet iced tea are the two drinks with next to none.

Does Waffle House sell its coffee to take home?

The merchandise section of the official Waffle House website is the place to check for take-home items. In summer 2025, a canned cold brew made from the Classic Blend was sold for a limited time at selected restaurants in Atlanta.
