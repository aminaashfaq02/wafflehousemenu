The Waffle House “Secret Menu”: 8 Custom Orders Worth Asking For

Waffle House does not have an official secret menu. What it has is a grill where almost everything is cooked to order, which means many changes are possible if you ask clearly and the cook is willing. These are eight custom orders built from regular menu items, with the calories for each and a few tips on how to ask.

WG
By Editorial Team
Waffle Guide

The short answer

There is no official secret menu. “Secret menu” orders are combinations of regular items, such as hashbrowns all the way, a waffle with both chocolate and peanut butter chips, or cheese eggs. Whether an off-menu request is made is up to the cook, and extras are usually charged.

How the “secret menu” really works

Everything at Waffle House is cooked on the grill in front of you, from the eggs to the melts. Because the kitchen works from individual ingredients (the same eggs, cheese, meats and toppings go into dozens of dishes), it is often possible to add or swap something.

If an item is not on the menu, though, the cook decides whether to make it. The best “secret” orders are simple changes to things that are already on the menu.

8 custom orders to ask for

1. Hashbrowns all the way

Every topping on a regular order: onions, cheese, ham, tomatoes, jalapeños, mushrooms, chili and sausage gravy.

  • About 520 calories, 22g of protein and 2,970mg of sodium.
  • On the menu as “all the way”, about $11.95.

2. Double-chip waffle

A classic waffle with both chocolate chips and peanut butter chips.

  • About 670 calories, 12g of protein and 965mg of sodium.
  • Ask for both toppings; expect to pay for each.

3. Cheese eggs

Scrambled eggs with a slice of American cheese per egg, an option you can add to any breakfast.

  • About 280 calories, 19g of protein and 625mg of sodium.
  • Add to any egg breakfast.

4. Biscuit, gravy and an egg

Biscuit and sausage gravy with a scrambled egg on top.

  • About 560 calories, 12g of protein and 1,870mg of sodium.
  • Ask to add an egg.

5. Peppered patty melt

The Texas Angus Patty Melt with jalapeños added.

  • About 740 calories, 26g of protein and 1,550mg of sodium.
  • Add jalapeños to the melt.

6. Bacon cheesesteak melt

The Texas Cheesesteak Melt with bacon added, which some restaurants list as its own item.

  • About 740 calories, 33g of protein and 1,750mg of sodium.
  • About $10.35 where it is listed.

7. Waffle “burger”

An Angus patty and cheese served with a waffle in place of the bun, a request that has done the rounds online.

  • About 680 calories, 26g of protein and 1,175mg of sodium.
  • Not a menu item; it is up to the cook.

8. Triple hashbrowns, scattered, smothered and covered

A triple order with onions and cheese for sharing.

  • About 645 calories, 11g of protein and 980mg of sodium.
  • A triple order is about $4.85 before toppings.

How to ask for an off-menu order

  1. Order it as changes to a menu item (“a patty melt with jalapeños”) rather than by a name you saw online.
  2. Keep it simple during busy times; one or two changes are easier than a whole new dish.
  3. Expect to pay for extras such as cheese, toppings and added meat.
  4. If the cook says no, take it gracefully: it is their grill.
