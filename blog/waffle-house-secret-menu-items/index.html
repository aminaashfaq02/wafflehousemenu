Quick Answer: What Is the Waffle House Secret Menu?

While Waffle House has no official printed “secret menu,” short-order grill masters can craft almost any custom order using in-house ingredients. Iconic secret items include the Waffle Burger (Angus burger patties between sweet cream waffles), the Texas Bacon Cheesesteak Melt, the Pecan Waffle Sandwich, and “All the Way” loaded bowls. Items typically cost between $4.50 and $11.50. For standard menu rates, explore our complete Waffle House menu and prices guide.

Independent Consumer Guide: WaffleHouse-Menu.com is an independent informational resource not affiliated with Waffle House, Inc. Secret menu preparations and customization willingness vary by franchise location, grill operator, and diner volume.

Top 10 Legendary Waffle House Secret Menu Items

Because Waffle House prepares every meal in open sight on an expansive flat-top griddle, the chain is famously customizable. Diners and late-night regulars have spent decades inventing creative culinary hacks. Here are the 10 most popular, restaurant-tested secret menu creations:

01

The Viral Waffle Burger (Double Angus)

Viral Fan Favorite

This internet sensation replaces ordinary hamburger buns with two warm, crispy sweet cream waffles. The balance of savory grilled beef, melted American cheese, and rich sweet waffle creates an unforgettable sweet-and-savory flavor profile.

How to Order: “Can I get a Double Cheeseburger, but substitute the buns with two sweet cream waffles?” (Or order a waffle cut in half if you prefer a single waffle).
02

The Texas Bacon Cheesesteak Melt

Savory Powerhouse

Takes the standard Texas Cheesesteak Melt to the next level by chopping three strips of crispy smoked bacon directly into the grilled ribeye and caramelized onions before sealing it between two buttery slices of grilled Texas toast.

How to Order: “Texas Cheesesteak Melt on Texas toast with grilled onions, double American cheese, and add three strips of bacon grilled into the meat.”
03

The Pecan Waffle Breakfast Sandwich

Southern Classic Hack

Combining Southern toasted Georgia pecans with savory breakfast meats. The nutty crunch of the toasted pecans baked into the sweet waffle batter pairs exceptionally well with salty bacon or a savory pork sausage patty.

How to Order: “One Pecan Waffle, two basted or scrambled eggs, and three strips of bacon. Please serve the meat and eggs inside the waffle folded like a taco.”
04

The “All The Way” Platter Bowl

Extreme Diner Plate

Standard hashbrown melt bowls come with select toppings, but ordering a bowl “All The Way” commands the cook to combine all eight official toppings (onions, cheese, ham, tomatoes, jalapeños, mushrooms, Bert’s chili, and sausage gravy) over a double bed of crispy hashbrowns.

How to Order: “Can I get a Double Hashbrown Bowl with two sunny-side eggs, ordered All the Way with every topping?” (Check our Hashbrowns 8 Ways Guide for individual terminology).
05

The Grilled Chicken & Bacon Club Melt

High-Protein Melt

A delicious alternative to beef, using a whole marinated chicken breast chopped fine on the grill, smothered in caramelized onions, layered with crisp bacon, and sealed inside golden Texas toast with double American cheese.

How to Order: “Texas Grilled Chicken Melt on Texas toast, add three slices of bacon, and grill the onions extra caramelized.”

Secret Hashbrown Grill Calls & Preparation Hacks

Most diners only know the official 8 toppings (scattered, smothered, covered, etc.), but experienced short-order cooks recognize several unofficial preparation calls:

“In the Ring” (Thick Pancake Puck)

Instead of scattering the potato shreds across the griddle, the cook leaves them compressed inside the metal cooking ring. The result is a thick, fluffy potato patty with crispy golden tops and a creamy, hash-like interior.

“Scattered Well-Done” (Dark Crunch)

If you dislike any soft potato texture, this command tells the grill operator to leave your potatoes on the scorching steel surface for an extra 3 to 4 minutes until both sides shatter with deep golden crunch.

“Steamed Under the Dome” (Low Oil)

Health-conscious guests can ask for their hashbrowns steamed. The cook avoids ladle oil, covers the potatoes with a stainless melting dome, and melts an ice cube to cook the potatoes tender with 60% less fat.

“On a Separate Platter”

When ordering combos like the All-Star Special, ask for hashbrowns on a separate oval platter so pancake syrup from your waffle does not soak into your savory crispy potatoes.

Sweet Waffle & Dessert Secret Combinations

Waffle House sweet cream waffle batter has remained virtually unchanged for over 65 years. Here is how clever diners create bakery-worthy desserts using diner ingredients:

1. The Peanut Butter Cup Waffle (Dual-Chip)

Ask your server for a waffle with both chocolate chips and peanut butter chips mixed into the batter. The chips melt simultaneously on the 400°F iron, creating an authentic warm Reese’s Peanut Butter Cup effect.

2. Apple Pie Waffle a la Mode

Order a hot waffle and a slice of Southern Pecan Pie or warm fruit topping. Place the warm pie slice directly onto the center of the waffle, allowing the brown sugar pecan glaze to melt into the waffle pockets.

3. The Grilled Cinnamon Raisin Toast Hack

Order cinnamon raisin toast “grilled extra dark on the butter griddle.” The surface sugars caramelize into a crisp, caramelized crust resembling a toasted French beignet.

The 4 Golden Rules: How to Order Secret Menu Items Politely

Waffle House grill operators are world-renowned for their high-speed ticket calling system (using condiment packets placed on plates to symbolize orders). To ensure your custom creation is received with a smile, keep these four tips in mind:

  1. Describe the Ingredients, Don't Use Internet Nicknames: While cooks know what “scattered well-done” means, newer staff might not know what a “Waffle House McGriddle” or “TikTok Secret Melt” is. Simply explain the build: “Can I get a Texas Cheesesteak with bacon added?”
  2. Avoid the Peak Weekend Breakfast Rush: Between 8:30 AM and 1:00 PM on Saturdays and Sundays, kitchen lines are at maximum capacity. Ordering complex off-menu creations when a diner has a 30-minute line can disrupt ticket flow. Late afternoon, late evening, or weekday shifts are ideal.
  3. Expect Modest Add-On Charges: Waffle House registers account for add-ons like extra bacon slices ($1.25), extra cheese ($0.60), or specialty waffle batters. Don't expect custom substitutions to be free.
  4. Tip Your Grill Master Generously: Waffle House operates with an open-kitchen theater. When your cook puts extra effort into tailoring a custom waffle burger or perfectly crispy hashbrowns, a generous tip in the counter jar shows genuine appreciation.
