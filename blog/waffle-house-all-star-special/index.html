Waffle House All-Star Special: What's in It, How to Order It, and What to Expect

By Waffle House Menu Editorial Team • Menu Item Guide
Quick Summary • All-Star Special
• What it is: A five-piece breakfast combo with eggs, meat, waffle, toast, and a side.
• Available hours: Served 24 hours a day at most locations across the United States.
• Customization: Egg style, breakfast meat, bread type, and side are completely customizable.
• Pricing: Prices vary by store and market. Check your local restaurant menu for current rates.

The Waffle House All-Star Special is the most complete combo plate you can get at Waffle House. It combines five separate breakfast staples into one bundled order: two eggs, a breakfast meat, a sweet cream waffle, toast, and a side of hashbrowns or grits. If you want a hearty breakfast without having to piece together separate side orders, this plate gives you everything at once.

Because prices vary by location and change over time, the exact amount you pay depends on where you stop. You can always check the posted menu board at your local diner to confirm current rates before ordering.

What Is the Waffle House All-Star Special?

The All-Star Special is Waffle House's flagship breakfast combination. Rather than picking eggs on one ticket, a waffle on another, and hashbrowns on the side, this meal bundles all the diner's core breakfast favorites together.

It has been a mainstay on the menu for decades because it satisfies people with big appetites. Whether you stop in during an early morning commute or after a late-night drive, the combo is served around the clock. If you want to compare it against other diner platters, browse our full Waffle House menu and item guide.

What Comes in the Waffle House All-Star Special

When your plate lands on the counter, it includes five distinct elements. Each part can be tailored to how you like your breakfast cooked:

COMBO COMPONENT PREPARATION & DETAILS
Two Eggs Cooked to order on the grill. Popular choices include scrambled with cheese, sunny side up, over easy, over medium, or basted.
Breakfast Meat Your choice among crispy bacon strips, savory sausage patties, or a grilled slice of country ham.
Sweet Cream Waffle The signature golden waffle baked in commercial irons with sweet cream batter, served hot with butter and warm syrup.
Toast with Jelly Two slices of grilled white bread, whole wheat, or cinnamon raisin toast with melted butter and fruit jelly packets.
Side: Hashbrowns or Grits A portion of shredded hashbrowns griddled crispy or a warm bowl of Southern grits with butter.

Having both a warm waffle and savory eggs on the same ticket is what makes the meal so distinct. You get sweet and savory balance without having to choose one over the other.

How to Customize the All-Star Special

One of the best things about eating at Waffle House is how flexible the grill cook can be with your order. You can personalize every single item:

Eggs: You aren't stuck with basic fried eggs. You can request them scrambled light, scrambled well, scrambled with melted cheese, over easy, or basted. Basted eggs are steamed under a small metal cover with water on the grill, creating silky whites and a warm runny yolk without flipping.

Meat: Choose bacon, pork sausage patties, or country ham. If you prefer a leaner bite, ask about chicken sausage patties if they are carried at your local restaurant.

Bread: White toast is standard, but you can request whole wheat or raisin toast. Grilled Texas toast is also common upon request.

Hashbrowns: If you select hashbrowns over grits, you can take advantage of the classic ordering lingo. Ask for them "smothered" for grilled sweet onions or "covered" for melted American cheese slices. You can also ask for jalapeños, mushrooms, chili, or gravy. Keep in mind that extra toppings usually add a small add-on charge.

Helpful Ordering Tips for First-Time Diners

Waffle House runs on an open grill format. Servers call tickets aloud and cooks track orders visually using jelly packets and condiments placed on plates. Here are tips to order smoothly:

State your choices upfront. When the server asks for your order, give all five specifications at once: egg style, meat, side, bread, and any toppings. For example: "I'll have the All-Star Special with two basted eggs, bacon, hashbrowns smothered and covered, and wheat toast." This gives the server everything needed in one go.

Request your waffle timing if needed. If you like to finish your eggs and savory hashbrowns first before having your hot waffle as dessert, you can politely mention that to your server.

Save with coupons and specials. Joining the brand's customer email club frequently yields a welcome coupon for a complimentary waffle. You can check current ways to save in our Waffle House coupons and discount guide.

Review dietary information in advance. If you are managing sodium, carbohydrates, or food sensitivities, check our Waffle House calories and allergen breakdown or review the diner dietary and keto guide before your visit.
