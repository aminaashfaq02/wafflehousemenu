Waffle House All-Star Special: What's in It, Calories & Price Guide

Five classic breakfast items on a single ticket make this the most frequently ordered plate on the open grill. Breaking down the custom calls, meat options, and price factors makes it an even better value.

Short answer

The Waffle House All-Star Special bundles five distinct items: two eggs cooked to order, choice of three bacon strips, two sausage patties, or country ham, a classic sweet cream waffle, toast with jelly, and either crispy hashbrowns or Southern grits.

It is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week at almost every location nationwide. Pricing changes over time and varies by location, but bundling these components together typically saves money compared to ordering each one individually a la carte.

This is an independent information site and is not affiliated with Waffle House, Inc. All menu details, prices, and food items vary by location and change over time.

The Waffle House All-Star Special is the most complete combo plate you can get at Waffle House. It brings together five separate breakfast staples into one bundled order. If you want a hearty breakfast without having to piece together individual side orders, this plate gives you everything at once.

Because prices vary by location and change over time, the exact amount you pay depends on where you stop. You can always check the posted menu board at your local diner to confirm current rates before ordering. For full diner listings, consult our full Waffle House menu and item guide.

What Comes in the Waffle House All-Star Special

When your plate arrives at the counter or booth, it includes five distinct elements. Each part can be tailored to how you like your breakfast cooked:

COMBO COMPONENT PREPARATION & DETAILS
Two Eggs Cooked to order on the grill. Popular choices include scrambled with cheese, sunny side up, over easy, over medium, or basted.
Breakfast Meat Your choice among crispy bacon strips, savory sausage patties, or a grilled slice of country ham.
Sweet Cream Waffle The signature golden waffle baked in commercial irons with sweet cream batter, served hot with butter and warm syrup.
Toast with Jelly Two slices of grilled white bread, whole wheat, or cinnamon raisin toast with butter and fruit jelly packets.
Side: Hashbrowns or Grits A portion of shredded hashbrowns griddled crispy or a warm bowl of Southern grits with butter.

How to Customize the All-Star Special

One of the best things about eating at Waffle House is how flexible the grill cook can be with your order. You can personalize every single item:

Eggs: You aren't stuck with basic fried eggs. You can request them scrambled light, scrambled well, scrambled with melted cheese, over easy, or basted. Basted eggs are steamed under a small metal cover with water on the grill, creating silky whites and a warm runny yolk without flipping.

Meat: Choose bacon, pork sausage patties, or country ham. If you prefer a leaner bite, ask about chicken sausage patties if they are carried at your local restaurant.

Bread: White toast is standard, but you can request whole wheat or raisin toast. Grilled Texas toast is also common upon request.

Hashbrown Ordering Lingo: Custom Topping Options

If you choose hashbrowns over grits as your side, you can order them using the restaurant's iconic ordering vocabulary. Toppings can be ordered individually or combined:

STYLE TERM WHAT IS ADDED TO THE HASHBROWNS
Scattered Spread out across the flat-top grill for maximum golden crispiness (the standard preparation).
Smothered Sautéed with sweet onions for rich aroma and flavor.
Covered Topped with melted slices of American cheese.
Chunked Tossed with chunks of grilled hickory-smoked city ham.
Peppered Spiced up with pickled jalapeño pepper slices.
Capped Grilled with sliced white button mushrooms.
All the Way Every topping combined onto one hearty plate. Note that extra toppings carry modest individual add-on charges.

Nutrition & Calorie Perspectives Across Selections

Because the All-Star Special allows you to pick your meat, bread, and side, the total calorie profile depends heavily on which items you select. Here is how choices compare in general terms:

SELECTION CATEGORY NUTRITIONAL PERSPECTIVE & TIPS
Meat Choice Bacon strips generally offer a lighter calorie choice than pork sausage patties. Country ham provides a hearty portion with higher natural sodium content.
Side: Hashbrowns vs. Grits Plain grits cooked in water with a pat of butter generally represent a lighter carbohydrate side compared to shredded potatoes griddled in cooking oil.
Egg Preparation Basted or poached-style eggs steam on the grill with water, using less surface oil than scrambled eggs mixed with melted cheese slices.
Waffle Toppings Syrup and butter account for a significant portion of total sweetness. Asking for syrup on the side lets you control how much is added.

Waffle House All-Star Special Price & Value Factors

Menu prices at diner restaurants are never completely identical from city to city. What you pay in a quiet rural town may differ from what a highway rest stop or metropolitan restaurant charges.

PRICE FACTOR HOW IT INFLUENCES YOUR CHECK
Regional Operating Costs Labor, utilities, and commercial lease costs differ by state and municipality, which creates regional price variations.
Location Type Travel plazas and interstate exits with high turnaround often have slightly different menu pricing than neighborhood diners.
Ingredient Add-Ons Adding extra cheese to your eggs, upgrading to a specialty waffle, or adding multiple hashbrown toppings will adjust the final total.
Combo Bundling Savings Purchasing these five items together as the All-Star Special offers substantial savings compared to ordering each one individually a la carte.

How to Order the All-Star Special Like a Pro

1

Decide your eggs before the server walks over: Because the diner moves fast, stating your egg style, meat, bread, and side in one breath keeps the order moving smoothly.

2

Ask for your waffle crisp if you love crunch: Requesting your waffle cooked slightly darker or crispier gives the edges an extra snap without changing the batter.

3

Time your waffle for dessert: If you prefer to eat your savory eggs, bacon, and hashbrowns first while hot, ask your server to hold the waffle iron call for a few minutes.

4

Split the plate to stretch your dollar: The All-Star Special is generous enough for two lighter eaters. Adding a single extra side waffle or coffee easily feeds two.

5

Check for welcome club coupons: Joining the customer email club frequently provides a coupon for a complimentary waffle. Learn more in our deals and coupons guide.
