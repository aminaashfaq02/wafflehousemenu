Waffle House Hashbrowns: Every Topping, the Lingo, Prices & Calories

Waffle House hashbrowns come regular, large or triple. A regular order is typically about $3.45 and 190 calories. Each topping usually adds about $1.00: smothered (onions), covered (cheese), chunked (ham), diced (tomatoes), peppered (jalapeños), capped (mushrooms), topped (chili) and country (sausage gravy). Order them all and they are “all the way”.

Typical prices compiled by Waffle Guide on November 4, 2026. Prices vary by location.

Hashbrowns are the side most people build their Waffle House order around, and they come with their own vocabulary. Scattered, smothered, covered, chunked: each word adds something specific. Here is what every term means, what the sizes and toppings cost, and how the calories change as you add them.

A regular order comes with breakfast plates such as the All-Star Special, and the breakfast hashbrown bowls are built on a large order. Plain hashbrowns list soy as their only major allergen.

The eight toppings and what to call them

Hashbrowns are cooked “scattered”, spread thin across the grill so more of them crisps. Each topping then has its own word:

SayYou getCalories addedSodium added
SmotheredGrilled onions+15+0mg
CoveredSlice of American cheese+50+250mg
ChunkedHickory smoked ham+60+500mg
DicedGrilled tomatoes+5+0mg
PepperedJalapeño peppers+10+390mg
CappedGrilled mushrooms+20+280mg
ToppedBert’s Chili+80+380mg
CountrySausage gravy+90+910mg

Stack the words in any order: “scattered, smothered and covered” (onions and cheese) is about 255 calories on a regular order. “All the way” means every topping on the list, about 520 calories and 2,970mg of sodium.

Other ways to have them cooked

  • Well-done: left on the grill longer for a darker, crunchier finish.
  • Cooked dry: made without the usual oil on the grill.
  • Steamed: covered and cooked with a little ice so they steam rather than fry. Not every cook offers it, so ask.
These are cooking requests rather than menu items, so how they turn out depends on the cook. The nutrition figures on this page are for a standard order.

Lighter ways to order them

  • Grilled onions (15 calories) and diced tomatoes (5) add flavour for almost nothing.
  • Sausage gravy (90 calories, 910mg sodium) and chili (80 calories) add the most.
  • Ask for them cooked dry to skip the usual grill oil.
  • Swap them for grits (90 calories) or sliced tomatoes (10) on a breakfast plate.
