Waffle House Thanksgiving Hours 2026: Is Waffle House Open on Thanksgiving?

While most restaurant chains turn off their grills for the holiday, Waffle House keeps its doors unlocked. Understanding peak rush hours, menu availability, and whole pie to-go orders makes holiday dining smooth.

By Waffle House Menu Editorial Team • Holiday Dining Guide

Short answer

Yes, Waffle House is open on Thanksgiving Day. Almost every location across the country operates on its regular 24-hour schedule, 365 days a year.

The full standard menu—including waffles, hashbrowns, breakfast steaks, and melts—is available around the clock. Morning hours before noon and late afternoon between 6:00 PM and 10:00 PM represent the heaviest customer rushes.

This is an independent information site and is not affiliated with Waffle House, Inc. Operating hours, menu availability, and holiday staffing can vary by location.

If you are wondering whether you can get a hot meal on Thanksgiving Day without turning on an oven, the answer is yes. Waffle House Thanksgiving hours are identical to its normal hours: the vast majority of restaurants are open 24 hours a day.

Whether you need a quick breakfast before heading over to family, a late-night bite after a long road trip, or an escape from a kitchen catastrophe, the yellow sign stays lit. You can look up your nearest location using our Waffle House store hours and locations directory.

Thanksgiving Day Operating Schedule & Rush Windows

While doors stay open around the clock, foot traffic on Thanksgiving follows very distinct patterns. Knowing when the counter fills up helps you pick the easiest time to visit:

TIME WINDOW TRAFFIC LEVEL WHAT TO EXPECT AT THE DINER
Early Morning (12 AM – 7 AM) Light to Moderate Easy seating, calm atmosphere, and quick service for early risers and overnight travelers.
Breakfast Rush (8 AM – 12 PM) Peak Rush The busiest window of the holiday. Expect waiting lines for booths as families gather for breakfast before cooking Thanksgiving dinner.
Mid-Afternoon (1 PM – 4 PM) Calm & Steady Most people are eating home-cooked turkey dinners, making this one of the quietest times to get a booth.
Late Evening (7 PM – 11 PM) Moderate to Heavy Customers seeking pie, late-night hashbrowns, or post-football snacks create steady second-shift traffic.

What Menu Items Are Served on Thanksgiving?

Unlike some casual dining restaurants that offer a special fixed-price Thanksgiving turkey dinner, Waffle House sticks to what it does best: classic diner comfort food. You can order the standard everyday menu throughout the day:

MENU CATEGORY AVAILABILITY HOLIDAY HIGHLIGHTS
Breakfast Combos & Eggs Full Regular Menu The All-Star Special is the top seller, providing eggs, meat, waffle, toast, and potatoes in one meal.
Golden Sweet Cream Waffles Full Regular Menu Classic sweet cream waffles, pecan waffles, and chocolate chip waffles served warm with real butter and syrup.
Custom Hashbrowns Full Regular Menu Ordered scattered, smothered (onions), covered (cheese), chunked (ham), or all the way with chili and mushrooms.
Texas Melts & Burgers Full Regular Menu Texas Patty Melts, Cheesesteak Melts, and Angus burgers served hot off the grill for lunch or dinner.
Holiday Pies High Demand Southern Pecan Pie and Triple Chocolate Silk Pie by the slice or boxed whole to take home.

The Southern Pecan Pie Tradition on Thanksgiving

One of the lesser-known holiday customs at Waffle House is picking up whole pies for the holiday dessert table. If you don't have time to bake from scratch, Waffle House sells its signature Southern Pecan Pie and Triple Chocolate Silk Pie boxed and ready to go.

Because whole pies sell out quickly on Thanksgiving morning, it is a good idea to speak with your local manager a few days ahead to ensure an extra boxed pie is set aside for you. For event catering details, review our Waffle House catering and group orders guide.

Dine-In vs. Holiday Takeout & To-Go Orders

During the intense morning rush, open booths turn over fast. If you are debating between sitting at the counter or taking food home, here is how each option works on a holiday:

DINING FORMAT PROS & BENEFITS HOLIDAY CONSIDERATIONS
Dine-In Counter or Booth Food arrives piping hot straight from the grill iron. Festive holiday diner atmosphere. Wait times can be 15 to 30 minutes during the 9 AM to 11 AM peak breakfast rush.
Call-In or Digital To-Go Take breakfast home to family without waiting for an open table. Standard to-go packaging surcharges apply. Phone lines may ring busy during heavy rushes.

How to Plan Your Thanksgiving Visit Like a Pro

1

Aim for the early morning lull: Getting to the diner between 6:30 AM and 8:00 AM lets you sit immediately, enjoy relaxed service, and finish breakfast well before holiday traffic builds.

2

Tip your holiday team generously: The grill cooks and servers are working hard on a national holiday so customers can eat. Extra generosity on your tip is a wonderful way to say thank you.

3

Order in one breath: When seats are full, servers appreciate clear orders: egg style, meat choice, side, and bread. Review our All-Star Special ordering guide to have your choices ready.

4

Check pricing and deals before you travel: Regional rates vary across states. Compare rates in our Waffle House prices by state breakdown and check for loyalty perks in our coupons guide.
