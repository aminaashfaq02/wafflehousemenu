Is Waffle House Open on Thanksgiving? Hours, Menu & Pie

Yes. All of Waffle House’s more than 2,000 restaurants are open 24 hours a day, seven days a week, Thanksgiving included. The regular menu is served all day. A single restaurant sometimes closes for local reasons such as staffing or severe weather, so check the official locator or call ahead if you are driving a long way.

If the oven is full, the plans fell through or you are on the road, Waffle House is one of the few places reliably open on Thanksgiving. Here is what to expect on the day, what to order and how to check your local restaurant is open before you go.

Waffle House hours on Thanksgiving

Waffle House restaurants are open 24 hours a day, seven days a week. There are no separate holiday hours: a restaurant open at 3 a.m. on a Tuesday is normally open at 3 a.m. on Thanksgiving too.

The exceptions are local. A restaurant sometimes closes for a short time because of staffing, repairs or a storm. It is rare, but it happens. If you are planning a special trip, look up the restaurant on the official Waffle House locator and call it.

Expect it to be busier than a normal weekday, especially around breakfast. Waffle House does not publish wait times, so there is no reliable way to predict how long you will wait.

What is on the menu

The regular menu, all day: waffles, hashbrowns, egg breakfasts, the All-Star Special, Texas melts, burgers and dinners. There is no turkey dinner or special Thanksgiving menu, so plan on the usual favourites. See everything with typical prices on the full menu.

Pie on Thanksgiving

Waffle House serves two pies by the slice: Southern Pecan Pie (about $4.45, 520 calories) and Triple Chocolate Pie (about $4.45, 880 calories). Whether a restaurant will sell a whole pie is up to the restaurant, so ask in advance if you want one for the table at home.

Both pies list milk, wheat and soy, and the pecan pie also lists egg and tree nuts. See the allergen chart for details.

Christmas, New Year and other holidays

The same applies to other holidays. Because Waffle House restaurants are open 24 hours a day, seven days a week, they are normally open on Christmas Eve, Christmas Day, New Year’s Eve and New Year’s Day, with the same local exceptions.

This round-the-clock reputation is also behind the informal “Waffle House Index”, which emergency managers have used to judge how hard a storm has hit an area. Read more on the locations page.

How to plan your visit

  1. Find the restaurant on the official locator and check it is open.
  2. Go early or late in the day if you want to avoid the busiest stretch.
  3. Order a to-go meal by phone if you would rather eat at home. Ask the restaurant about any to-go charges.
  4. Bring your patience and tip your server: holidays are among the busiest days of the year for the crew.
