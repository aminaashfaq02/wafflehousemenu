Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about redeeming promo codes and discounts at Waffle House.

For in-person dining, present your printed coupon or show the digital barcode on your smartphone to your server or cashier when settling your guest ticket. For online merchandise purchases on the official Waffle House store, enter your promo code into the "Discount Code" box at checkout.
Official Regulars Club vouchers and national promotions are honored at all participating corporate and franchised Waffle House restaurants across the United States. However, local community discounts (such as senior, student, and local law enforcement discounts) are set at the discretion of individual restaurant management.
While there is no blanket national corporate policy, the vast majority of local diner managers proudly offer a 10% discount to active-duty military personnel, veterans, first responders, and senior citizens (typically 60+). Simply present your military ID, uniform, or senior credentials to your server upon ordering.
Sign up for the free Waffle House Regulars Club on wafflehouse.com and provide your date of birth. During your birthday month, you will automatically receive an email voucher redeemable for a free waffle or slice of Southern pie with the purchase of any drink or meal.
Standard diner policy permits one coupon or promotional offer per table or guest check. You can, however, take advantage of the combo pricing on the All-Star Special™ or free coffee refills alongside an individual discount code if approved by your location.
