Waffle House Coupons & Deals (2026)

This page does not list promo codes: none of the Waffle House codes we found online could be confirmed as working. Instead, here are the ways to save that hold up, starting with the free Regulars Club email program. Offers change and can vary by location.

Ways to Save at Waffle House

No codes to copy. These are the savings options you can check for yourself.

Free to join

Join the Regulars Club

The Regulars Club is Waffle House’s free email program. Members have been offered a sign-up coupon (reported as free hashbrowns), a birthday waffle and occasional deals by email. Sign up on the Waffle House website and check the current terms there.

Combo plate

Order the All-Star Special

The All-Star Special bundles eggs, a breakfast meat, hashbrowns or grits, toast and a waffle on one plate, which usually costs less than ordering each item separately. See our All-Star Special price guide for typical 2026 prices.

Ask locally

Ask About Local Discounts

Discounts for seniors, military or first responders are set by individual restaurants, if they are offered at all. Ask your server before you order.

How to Spend Less Without a Coupon

Using typical 2026 prices (they vary by location), these are the orders that give the most food for the money.

The All-Star Special is the best-value plate

Ordered separately, a two egg breakfast with bacon ($9.50) plus a classic waffle ($5.55) comes to about $15.05. The All-Star Special puts the same food on one plate for about $12.35, a saving of roughly $2.70.

Watch the hashbrown toppings

Each topping usually adds about $1.00. A regular order of hashbrowns is about $3.45, while scattered, smothered and covered is about $5.65, and ordering them all the way is about $11.95.

Prices change by location

The same order can cost a few dollars more near highways, airports and in bigger cities. Compare typical prices and price ranges on the prices by state page before you go.
