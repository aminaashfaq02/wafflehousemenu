Master Allergen Matrix & Kitchen Safety Disclosures

Waffle House utilizes shared kitchen equipment, waffle bakers, and griddles. Even items that do not list an allergen as an active ingredient may encounter trace cross-contact during preparation.

Egg Eggs & Dairy (Milk)

Present in all sweet cream waffles, scrambled/fried eggs, omelets, sausage gravy, and American cheese slices.

Wheat Wheat & Gluten

Present in all waffles, Texas toast, buns, biscuits, sausage gravy, and chili base recipes.

Peanut Peanuts & Tree Nuts

Pecan waffles and peanut butter chip waffles use specialized dispensers; tell your server before ordering if you have nut allergies.

!️

Note for severe allergies: Waffle House does not certify any menu item as gluten-free or allergen-free due to shared prep tables. Always inform your server and cook about severe allergies prior to placing your order.
