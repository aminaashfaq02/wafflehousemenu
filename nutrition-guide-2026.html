Paper & Printing Best Practices

Standard Paper Formatting (US Letter & A4)

In your computer or mobile print dialog, set the orientation to Landscape and choose “Fit to printable area”. This prevents margin clipping and keeps all 12 columns perfectly centered across the sheet.

Double-Sided Printing & Ink Conservation

If printing double-sided, select “Flip on short edge” for landscape layouts. To save color cartridge ink, select “Black and White / Grayscale” — all borders and numbers maintain 100% legibility.

Group Orders & Road Trip Logistics

Printing Pages 1, 4, and 5 provides the essentials for group breakfast call-ins, tailgate catering, sports teams, and road-trippers passing through the Interstate diner corridor.
