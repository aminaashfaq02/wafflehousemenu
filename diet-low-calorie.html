What to watch for on the low-calorie menu

Calorie count is one consideration; sodium and cooking oil are another. Cured breakfast meats like country ham carry 2,110 mg of sodium per slice despite being under 200 calories. If you are watching blood pressure or water retention, opt for City Ham (850 mg sodium), Grilled Chicken Breast (470 mg sodium), or plain eggs (176 mg sodium). Always request eggs cooked “in the pan” with cooking spray to avoid up to 100 extra calories from liquid margarine on the flat top.

How this list is built

A dish qualifies at 600 calories or fewer. Dishes without official corporate disclosures or combo platters with unfixed sides are split into their verifiable components so you can calculate your custom meal.

Every data point mirrors the official Waffle House Nutrition Guide (May 2026 Edition), with zero synthetic estimates.

Low-Calorie Menu FAQ

Expert diner tips for calorie counting, macro tracking, and keeping sodium in check at Waffle House.

Order 2 farm fresh eggs cooked scrambled or poached (180 calories) with a side order of sliced tomatoes (10 calories) and an unsweet iced tea or black Royal Blend coffee (0-5 calories). That entire sit-down breakfast comes out to only 190–195 calories while delivering 12 grams of high-quality protein.
Politely request your server to have the grill operator prepare your eggs “in the pan with cooking spray” rather than dropping them directly onto the seasoned diner flat-top grill. Grill butter and residual bacon fat can add 50 to 100 unmeasured fat calories.
The Meat Lover's Chicken & Eggs platter (2 grilled chicken breasts and 2 whole eggs, plate only) supplies an astonishing 70 grams of protein for just 440 total calories and 16g of fat. It is one of the highest protein-to-calorie ratios in American casual dining.
Yes! A regular single portion of hashbrowns is 190 calories. You can ask for them prepared “steamed” or “crisped dry” to minimize cooking oil. Enhance flavor without massive calories by ordering them smothered (sautéed onions for +15 cal) and capped (grilled mushrooms for +10 cal).
Traditional diner curing methods use salt extensively for shelf-life and moisture retention. For instance, country ham contains 2,110 mg of sodium. Choosing City Ham (850 mg), bacon (375 mg for 3 slices), or fresh USDA Choice steaks (260–436 mg sodium) dramatically lowers your sodium intake while staying under your calorie limit.
Waffle House uses real shell eggs cracked fresh on the line. While liquid egg whites are not a standard stocked carton product at all locations, cooks can readily separate whole egg whites upon polite request during non-peak hours, bringing two egg whites down to approximately 35 calories and 7g protein.
