4 Nutrition, Calorie Calculations & Griddle Allergen Warnings

Our nutrition guides, PDF guides, and Hashbrown Bowl Calculator are constructed using verified historical nutritional disclosures, public ingredient audits, and official Waffle House nutritional fact sheets.

CRITICAL ALLERGEN CROSS-CONTACT NOTICE: Waffle House restaurants operate high-speed, open-view short-order griddles. Cooking surfaces, spatulas, waffle irons, and toasters are routinely shared during preparation of foods containing eggs, cow's milk, wheat (gluten), soy, peanuts, tree nuts, bacon grease, and shellfish. Cross-contact between ingredients can occur. Patrons with severe food allergies must exercise personal caution and alert their grill operator prior to cooking.

WaffleHouseMenu.us does not offer medical, nutritional, or clinical dietary advice. Calorie values represent standard preparation averages; diner customizations, butter applications, and cooking oil amounts can alter nutritional profiles.

Emergency Guidance: Our FEMA Index articles and storm tracking guides are published for historical diner culture and community educational interest only. During active hurricanes, tornadoes, ice storms, or emergency declarations:
