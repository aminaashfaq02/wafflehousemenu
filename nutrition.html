Which components list each major allergen, and how many of the 88 menu items include it. The full item-by-item chart is in the allergen chart.

Soy · 68 items

Tree nuts · 9 items

Listed for: Pecans, Southern Pecan Pie, Small Slice Southern Pecan Pie, Waffle.

Peanuts · 1 items

Listed for: Peanut Butter Chips.

Shared kitchen: Waffle House cooks on shared grills and waffle irons, so any item can come into contact with any allergen. The plain waffle lists tree nuts as well as the pecan waffle. With a severe allergy, confirm with the restaurant before ordering.

Frequently Asked Questions About Nutrition

Each dish is added up from its usual components, such as the waffle, eggs, toast, hashbrowns and meat, using per-component nutrition values compiled by our editorial team. Sides default to hashbrowns and white toast; changing them changes the totals.
Waffle House does not label items keto, but two scrambled eggs with bacon and sliced tomatoes come to about 3g of carbs. Steaks, pork chops, sausage and bacon have little or no carbohydrate; toast, waffles, biscuits, hashbrowns and grits carry most of it.
Two scrambled eggs (180 calories) with sliced tomatoes (10) and black coffee (5) come to about 195 calories and 14g of protein.
A classic waffle has about 410 calories, 18g of fat, 55g of carbohydrates and 8g of protein, before butter and syrup. Pecans add 150 calories, peanut butter chips 150 and chocolate chips 110.
Grilled onions and diced tomatoes add no measurable sodium. Cheese adds about 250mg, mushrooms 280mg, chili 380mg, jalapeños 390mg, ham 500mg and sausage gravy 910mg. A regular order of plain hashbrowns has about 240mg.
