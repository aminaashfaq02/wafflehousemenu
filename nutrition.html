Allergen Quick Reference

Waffle House diners use common preparation surfaces and equipment. See common allergen locations below.

Milk / Dairy

Appears in: American cheese slices, country gravies, sweet cream waffle batter, butter, and Texas toast melts.

Egg

Appears in: All farm-fresh eggs, sweet cream waffle batter, buttermilk biscuits, and fiesta omelets.

Wheat / Gluten

Appears in: White and wheat toast, buttermilk biscuits, sweet cream waffles, country sausage gravy, and burger buns.

Soy

Appears in: Flat-top grill oils, hashbrown seasonings, prepared sauces, and select sausage recipes.

Tree Nuts

Appears in: Georgia Pecan Waffle and Southern Pecan Pie.

Peanuts

Appears in: Peanut butter chip waffle toppings and peanut butter spread packets.

Important Shared Kitchen Notice Waffle House kitchens share cooking surfaces across items — always confirm allergen details directly with restaurant staff before ordering if you have a severe allergy.
