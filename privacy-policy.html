Welcome to Waffle Guide. Your privacy matters when you browse nutrition information, hashbrown prices and restaurant locations. This Privacy Policy explains how this site handles your data under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

1 Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to run this site. When we ask for personal information, we explain what it is and why at the point of collection:

  • Direct Inquiries & Price Corrections: If you reach out to us via our official contact forms or send receipt images to report local price fluctuations, we receive your name, email address, message contents, any attached photos and any other feedback you choose to send.
  • Interactive Tools: The Hashbrown Bowl Calculator and the menu filters run entirely in your browser. Your selections are not saved and are not sent to us. They are cleared when you leave or reload the page.
  • Automated Device & Network Telemetry: When you browse our guides, our servers automatically collect non-personally identifiable diagnostic data including browser user-agent strings, approximate geographical region (country or state level), operating system, referring URLs, and performance latency metrics.

2 How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for consumer advocacy, server stability, and diner content accuracy. Specifically, we use information to:

  • Operate & Maintain the Guide: Keep this site’s menu listings, 25-state price guides, and nutrition references available online.
  • Review Price Updates: Use the price updates readers send us when revising our price ranges.
  • Optimize Page Speed: Identify high-traffic spikes (such as during severe weather alerts when consumers monitor our Waffle House FEMA Index) to ensure web caching prevents server downtime.
  • Customer Support & Editorial Inquiries: Respond to corrections, broken links, or press requests.
  • Fraud & Abuse Prevention: Detect and mitigate malicious scraping bots, distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attempts, and comment spam.

3 Web Log Files & Standard Server Analytics

Waffle Guide follows standard web server logging protocols. These files log visitors when they access pages on our site. All web hosting providers perform this as an integral component of infrastructure health and network diagnostics.

The technical data captured in standard server log entries includes:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses (anonymized for analytical aggregation)
  • Browser software version and rendering engine (e.g. Chrome, Firefox, Safari)
  • Internet Service Provider (ISP) network identity
  • Date and time stamp of access
  • Referring website address and exit page headers
  • Aggregate number of HTTP requests and byte counts transferred

Note: None of this server diagnostic information is linked to personally identifiable data. We do not use server logs to trace individual diner patron identities or build commercial profiles.

4 Cookies, Web Beacons & Local Storage

Waffle Guide does not set cookies or use browser storage of its own. Cookies on the site come from Google Analytics and, where ads are shown, Google advertising services, as described in our Cookies Policy. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, these cookies stay off until you give consent.

The information is used solely to enhance your user experience by customizing our webpage content based on your browser category or screen resolution.

How to Manage or Disable Cookies

You have full autonomy to accept, reject, or delete cookies at any time through your personal browser settings:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Block all cookies.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Enhanced Tracking Protection.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies.

Note: If you disable cookies entirely, every guide, table and tool on the site still works.

6 California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA & CPRA) Rights

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents are guaranteed specific legal entitlements regarding their personal data:

  • Right to Know & Access: You have the right to ask us to disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal data we have collected about you over the preceding 12 months.
  • Right to Request Erasure: You have the right to ask us to permanently delete any personal information we have gathered directly from you, subject to legal compliance exceptions.
  • Right to Opt-Out of the Sale of Personal Data: We do NOT sell personal data. We have never sold consumer information and will never sell personal information in the future.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will never deny you menu access, charge differential rates, or degrade your user experience for exercising any of your statutory privacy rights.

If you submit a formal CCPA request, we are required by California law to respond within 45 days. To submit a request, please contact our privacy compliance desk at privacy@wafflehouse-menu.com.

7 General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) Rights

For visitors connecting from the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have these data protection rights under GDPR:

  • The Right to Access: You have the right to request official copies of any personal data stored about you.
  • The Right to Rectification: You have the right to ask us to correct any information you deem inaccurate or incomplete.
  • The Right to Erasure (The Right to be Forgotten): You have the right to request the complete deletion of your personal records under certain conditions.
  • The Right to Restrict Processing: You have the right to ask us to restrict the processing of your personal data under specific scenarios.
  • The Right to Object to Processing: You have the legal right to object to our processing of your personal data when conducted under legitimate interests.
  • The Right to Data Portability: You have the right to ask us to transfer collected records directly to another organization or to you in an open, machine-readable format.

If you make a GDPR inquiry, we have one calendar month to deliver an official response. Please email us at privacy@wafflehouse-menu.com with the subject header "GDPR Data Request".

8 Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

Protecting young diner fans on the internet is an uncompromised priority. We strongly encourage parents and guardians to observe, participate in, and supervise their children's online browsing activity.

Strict Under-13 Compliance: Waffle Guide does not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain any Personally Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If a parent or legal guardian believes their child has provided personal information through our contact form, please notify us immediately at privacy@wafflehouse-menu.com. We will delete any such information we hold, including the related emails.

9 Data Security Protocols & Retention Timelines

We implement industry-standard cybersecurity protections to preserve the confidentiality and integrity of our systems:

  • TLS 1.3 / SSL Encryption: All web requests across Waffle Guide are encrypted in transit via 256-bit Secure Socket Layer certificates.
  • Hosting Logs: The site is hosted by Vercel Inc., which keeps standard access logs (such as IP address, browser type and the page requested) for a limited period under its own privacy policy.
  • Zero Credential Storage: Because we do not provide user login accounts, no user passwords, credit card credentials, or bank accounts are ever stored on our servers.
