Welcome to WaffleHouseMenu.us. We understand that your privacy is fundamentally important when browsing nutritional information, hashbrown prices, and diner locations. This Privacy Policy is our binding commitment to treat your browsing experience with transparency, dignity, and high standard legal compliance under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

1 Information We Collect

We collect information to provide accurate, reliable, and up-to-date consumer reference services across all 25 states where Waffle House operates. The types of personal information that you are invited to provide, and the precise reasons why you are asked to provide it, are explained explicitly at the point of collection:

  • Direct Inquiries & Price Corrections: If you reach out to us via our official contact forms or send receipt images to report local price fluctuations, we may receive your name, email address, message contents, attached photos of physical diner menus, and any other feedback you voluntarily supply.
  • Interactive Tools & Local Calculations: When utilizing our Hashbrown Bowl Nutrition Calculator or filtering prices by state, your selected ingredients and states are stored client-side in your web browser session (LocalStorage/SessionStorage). This configuration data remains local to your device and is not saved to a user profile database.
  • Automated Device & Network Telemetry: When you navigate our diner tables, our servers automatically collect non-personally identifiable diagnostic data including browser user-agent strings, approximate geographical region (country or state level), operating system, referring URLs, and performance latency metrics.

2 How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for consumer advocacy, server stability, and diner content accuracy. Specifically, we use information to:

  • Operate & Maintain the Guide: Keep 54+ menu items, 25-state price matrices, and nutritional PDF references accessible 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
  • Verify Menu Price Fluctuations: Cross-reference crowdsourced diner receipts and operator notices against published state sales tax and wage changes.
  • Optimize Page Speed: Identify high-traffic spikes (such as during severe weather alerts when consumers monitor our Waffle House FEMA Index) to ensure web caching prevents server downtime.
  • Customer Support & Editorial Inquiries: Respond promptly within 24 to 48 hours to corrections, broken links, or press requests.
  • Fraud & Abuse Prevention: Detect and mitigate malicious scraping bots, distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attempts, and comment spam.

3 Web Log Files & Standard Server Analytics

WaffleHouseMenu.us follows standard web server logging protocols. These files log visitors when they access pages on our site. All web hosting providers perform this as an integral component of infrastructure health and network diagnostics.

The technical data captured in standard server log entries includes:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses (anonymized for analytical aggregation)
  • Browser software version and rendering engine (e.g. Chrome, Firefox, Safari)
  • Internet Service Provider (ISP) network identity
  • Date and time stamp of access
  • Referring website address and exit page headers
  • Aggregate number of HTTP requests and byte counts transferred

Crucial Transparency Guarantee: None of this server diagnostic information is linked to any data that is personally identifiable. We do not use server logs to trace individual diner patron identities or build commercial profiles.

4 Cookies, Web Beacons & Local Storage

Like almost every modern informational website, WaffleHouseMenu.us utilizes 'cookies' and browser local storage. These cookies are small text files placed on your device to store information such as visitor preferences, recently viewed state pricing tabs, and user navigation history.

The information is used solely to enhance your user experience by customizing our webpage content based on your browser category or screen resolution.

How You Can Manage or Disable Cookies

You have full autonomy to accept, reject, or delete cookies at any time through your personal browser settings:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Block all cookies.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Enhanced Tracking Protection.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies.

Note: If you disable cookies entirely, core static menu guides and nutrition tables will remain fully readable, though saved calculator tallies may reset on reload.

5 Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookies

Visitors retain the legal right to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google Ad and Content Network Privacy Policy at:

6 California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA & CPRA) Rights

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents are guaranteed specific legal entitlements regarding their personal data:

  • Right to Know & Access: You may request that we disclose the categories and specific pieces of personal data we have collected about you over the preceding 12 months.
  • Right to Request Erasure: You may request that we permanently delete any personal information we have gathered directly from you, subject to legal compliance exceptions.
  • Right to Opt-Out of the Sale of Personal Data: We do NOT sell personal data. We have never sold consumer information and will never sell personal information in the future.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will never deny you menu access, charge differential rates, or degrade your user experience for exercising any of your statutory privacy rights.

If you submit a formal CCPA request, we are required by California law to respond within 45 days. To submit a request, please contact our privacy compliance desk at privacy@wafflehousemenu.us.

7 General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) Rights

For visitors connecting from the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we ensure that you are fully empowered to exercise your statutory data protection rights under GDPR:

  • The Right to Access: You have the right to request official copies of any personal data stored about you.
  • The Right to Rectification: You have the right to request that we correct any information you deem inaccurate or incomplete.
  • The Right to Erasure (The Right to be Forgotten): You have the right to request the complete deletion of your personal records under certain conditions.
  • The Right to Restrict Processing: You have the right to demand that we restrict the processing of your personal data under specific scenarios.
  • The Right to Object to Processing: You have the legal right to object to our processing of your personal data when conducted under legitimate interests.
  • The Right to Data Portability: You have the right to request that we transfer collected records directly to another organization or to you in an open, machine-readable format.

If you make a GDPR inquiry, we have one calendar month to deliver an official response. Please email us at privacy@wafflehousemenu.us with the subject header "GDPR Data Request".

8 Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA)

Protecting young diner fans on the internet is an uncompromised priority. We strongly encourage parents and guardians to observe, participate in, and supervise their children's online browsing activity.

Strict Under-13 Compliance: WaffleHouseMenu.us does not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain any Personally Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If a parent or legal guardian believes that their child has provided personal information through our contact form, please notify us immediately at privacy@wafflehousemenu.us. We will take prompt action to immediately purge such records from our server logs and communication archives.

9 Data Security Protocols & Retention Timelines

We implement industry-standard cybersecurity protections to preserve the confidentiality and integrity of our systems:

  • TLS 1.3 / SSL Encryption: All web requests across WaffleHouseMenu.us are encrypted in transit via 256-bit Secure Socket Layer certificates.
  • Automated Rolling Log Purges: Diagnostic server access logs are systematically overwritten and permanently purged on a rolling 90-day retention schedule.
  • Zero Credential Storage: Because we do not provide user login accounts, no user passwords, credit card credentials, or bank accounts are ever stored on our servers.
