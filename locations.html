Hours & Storms

24/7 Operating Hours & The FEMA Waffle House Index

Most Waffle House restaurants are open 24 hours a day, every day of the year. Here is how that works, and why emergency managers have watched them during hurricanes.

Open Around the Clock

Most Waffle House restaurants stay open 24 hours a day, including most holidays. Individual restaurants can close for storms, repairs or staffing, so check your local listing.

  • ✓ Holidays: Most restaurants stay open on holidays, but hours can vary by location.
  • ✓ Late Night: Most restaurants serve the full menu overnight.
  • ✓ To-Go & Counter Seating: Both dine-in counter/booth service and takeout available round the clock.

The Waffle House FEMA Disaster Index

Coined by former FEMA Administrator Craig Fugate, the "Waffle House Index" is an informal metric used by emergency managers to gauge the severity of disasters based on diner operations:

Green
Level Green: Full Menu Restaurant open with its full menu.
Yellow
Level Yellow: Limited Menu Limited menu, for example because power, water or supplies are limited.
Red
Level Red: Diner Closed Restaurant closed, which signals severe damage in the area.
