1 What Are Cookies & Web Storage?

Cookies are small data files — typically consisting of letters and numbers — placed onto your desktop computer, tablet, or smartphone by websites you visit. They are widely implemented across the web to enable websites to function properly, remember user interface preferences, maintain security standards, and provide aggregated analytics to site publishers.

In addition to standard HTTP cookies, modern web platforms may utilize HTML5 LocalStorage and SessionStorage. Unlike cookies, client-side web storage holds data strictly within your local browser sandbox and is never automatically sent across the network with every HTTP request.

2 How We Use Cookies on WaffleHouseMenu.us

We operate an independent, community-driven food portal. We do not maintain customer login profiles or require user accounts. Consequently, we minimize cookie deployment to strictly what is necessary to deliver high-quality, free diner information:

  • Essential & Security Cookies: Necessary to route traffic efficiently through content delivery networks (CDNs), defend against malicious automated scraping traffic, and maintain site uptime during heavy weather spikes.
  • Functional Browser Storage: Remembering your active tab on the Prices by State page or retaining your custom ingredient tallies in our Hashbrown Bowl Calculator so your meal estimate is not lost when switching tabs.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: Helping us understand aggregated visitor behavior (such as which diner items receive the most search inquiries, or identifying broken links) through anonymized Google Analytics data.
  • Advertising Cookies: Third-party advertising networks (including Google AdSense) use cookies to display non-intrusive advertisements that fund our continuous price verification research across 25 U.S. states.

5 How to Manage or Disable Cookies in Your Browser

You retain the absolute technical right to block or delete cookies across any browser on any device. Step-by-step instructions for popular web browsers are detailed below:

Google Chrome (Desktop & Android) Navigate to Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data. From here you can block third-party cookies or purge stored site data.
Apple Safari (iOS & macOS) On macOS, go to Safari Preferences → Privacy → check "Block all cookies" or "Prevent cross-site tracking". On iOS, open Settings → Safari → toggle "Block All Cookies".
Mozilla Firefox Open Settings → Privacy & Security → select "Enhanced Tracking Protection" (Standard, Strict, or Custom) and manage cookie permissions.
Microsoft Edge Go to Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies and site data.

Please note: Disabling all cookies will NOT prevent you from viewing our full menu prices, nutritional guides, or state comparisons.
