Everything you need to know about Waffle House menu prices, calorie counts, and ordering.

The All-Star Special™ averages $12.60 at most U.S. locations. It includes two eggs, toast & jelly, choice of bacon, country sausage or city ham, a full order of crispy hashbrowns or grits, and a classic sweet cream waffle.
These are classic Waffle House hashbrown codes! “Scattered” means spread evenly across the flat-top grill for extra crispiness; “Smothered” adds sautéed onions; “Covered” melts sliced American cheese over the top. Other popular toppings include “Chunked” (ham) and “Peppered” (jalapeños).
Yes, almost all 1,900+ Waffle House diners nationwide are open 24 hours every day, including Thanksgiving, Christmas, and New Year's Day.
