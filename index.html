Got Questions? Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Waffle House prices, calories, and menu info.

Most full breakfast meals and combos at Waffle House range between $8.00 and $13.50, with popular staples like the All-Star Special averaging $10.45. Standalone sweet-cream waffles start around $4.60, while hearty custom hashbrown bowls typically cost between $7.50 and $9.50. Exact pricing may vary slightly across independent franchise locations and states.

Yes, Waffle House provides a dedicated kids menu tailored for children aged 10 and under. Young diner guests can choose from junior golden waffles with bacon, grilled cheeseburgers, crispy tenders, and scrambled egg platters with small hash brown portions. Prices for kid-friendly plates generally fall between $4.50 and $5.75.

Yes, many local Waffle House locations offer pickup catering and large group party platters for morning office events, weekend tailgates, and family gatherings. Catering favorites include build-your-own waffle stations, bulk scrambled egg and bacon trays, smothered hash brown boxes, and gallons of fresh diner coffee or iced tea. It is recommended to call your local diner a few days in advance to schedule large pickups.

While Waffle House is famous for steaks and bacon, there are multiple vegetarian-friendly diner options. Guests can enjoy classic or pecan waffles, farm eggs cooked to order, hot grits, grilled Texas toast, and scattered hash browns covered in melted American cheese, diced onions, grilled mushrooms, and jalapeños. Be sure to specify meat-free preparation on the grill when placing your order.

Waffle House locations operate 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, 365 days a year without closing. This includes all major holidays such as Christmas Day, Thanksgiving, New Year's Eve, and Easter. The diner chain's renowned resilience during extreme weather gave rise to the official FEMA "Waffle House Index" used by disaster response teams across the United States.
