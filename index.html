Frequently Asked Questions About the All-Star Special

Everything you need to know about Waffle House's signature breakfast plate, pricing, customization, and ordering.

The All-Star Special includes two eggs cooked any way you like, a choice of breakfast meat (bacon, sausage, or ham), a sweet cream waffle, toast with jelly, and either hashbrowns or grits. It gives you a complete breakfast plate in one order.
Prices vary by restaurant location and state. Diner operating costs and regional supply pricing affect menu rates. You can check the posted menu at your local Waffle House to see current prices.
Yes, customization is standard. You pick how your eggs are prepared, choose your meat, pick between hashbrowns or grits, and select your bread type. Hashbrown toppings like onions and cheese can also be added.
Yes. Most Waffle House locations run 24 hours a day and serve their full menu at all hours. You can order the All-Star Special early in the morning or late at night.
Bundling the five items into the combo typically saves money compared to ordering eggs, bacon, a waffle, toast, and hashbrowns a la carte. That combination value is why it remains so popular.
Waffle House does not serve pancakes. Every location uses sweet cream waffle irons. You can ask for your waffle to be cooked soft or crisp depending on how you like the crust.
