Waffle House Prices by State

Waffle House does not publish one national price list, so the same order can cost different amounts from one town or state to the next. This guide gives the typical price of each menu item and the range of prices seen across published restaurant price lists, so you know roughly what to expect before you order.

Why the price changes from place to place

Prices are set restaurant by restaurant rather than nationally. The reasons most often given for the differences are:

  • local wages and rent, which are higher in some cities and states;
  • restaurants beside highways, near airports or in tourist areas;
  • sales tax, which is added at the register and differs by state and city.

Frequently Asked Questions

The All-Star Special typically costs about $12.35, and published price lists show it from $10.30 to $14.10 depending on the restaurant. The price is the same whether you choose bacon, sausage or city ham.
Waffle House does not use one national price list, so prices are set restaurant by restaurant. Reasons commonly given for the differences are local wages and rent, and locations near highways, airports or tourist areas. Sales tax is added at the register and also differs by state and city.
A side of toast (about $2.45), a grilled biscuit (about $2.45) and a sausage egg and cheese biscuit (about $3.05) are among the lowest-priced items. A cup of coffee is about $2.65.
A classic waffle typically costs about $5.55 ($4.30 – $6.25 on published price lists). Pecan, chocolate chip and peanut butter chip waffles are about $6.50.
A regular order of hashbrowns is typically about $3.45 and a large order about $4.45. Each topping, such as onions or cheese, usually adds about $1.00.
