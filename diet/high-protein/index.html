High-Protein Menu FAQ

Pork Chops & Eggs has the most, at about 58g of protein and 890 calories. T-Bone & Eggs (54g) and Chicken Fiesta Protein Bowl (52g) are close behind.
The Chicken Fiesta Protein Bowl has about 52g of protein for 540 calories, the most protein of any dish under 600 calories.
About 36g with bacon, 38g with sausage and 43g with city ham, with hashbrowns and white toast.
Pork chops (two) have about 38g, a T-bone 34g, country ham 32g and grilled chicken 29g. Grilled chicken gives the most protein per calorie, at 140 calories.
Each extra scrambled egg adds about 6g of protein (90 calories), a slice of cheese 3g (50 calories), and a side of grilled chicken 29g (140 calories).
