Easy swaps to save calories

  • Grits instead of regular hashbrowns−100 cal
  • Sliced tomatoes instead of regular hashbrowns−180 cal
  • Wheat toast instead of white toast−10 cal
  • Raisin toast instead of white toast+70 cal
  • Leave off the toast−230 cal
  • Bacon instead of sausage−120 cal
  • City ham instead of sausage−150 cal
  • Each slice of American cheese+50 cal

Three lighter full plates

  • Two scrambled eggs, sliced tomatoes, wheat toast and black coffee415 cal · 22g protein
  • Grilled chicken, two scrambled eggs and sliced tomatoes330 cal · 43g protein
  • Classic waffle with a side of city ham520 cal · 23g protein

Estimates for each plate as listed. Jelly, butter and syrup are not included.

Low-Calorie Menu FAQ

Two scrambled eggs (180 calories) with sliced tomatoes (10) and black coffee (5) comes to about 195 calories. A bowl of grits is 90 calories, and a regular order of hashbrowns is 190.
The Chicken Fiesta Protein Bowl has about 52g of protein for 540 calories. A side of grilled chicken adds 29g of protein for 140 calories to any plate.
A regular order is about 190 calories, a large order 380 and a triple order 580. Toppings add from 5 calories (diced tomatoes) to 90 (sausage gravy), so scattered, smothered and covered comes to about 255.
City ham has about 110 calories, bacon (three slices) 140, country ham 210 and two sausage patties 260. Country ham is also the saltiest at about 1,720mg of sodium.
White toast adds about 230 calories, wheat toast 220 and raisin toast 300. Asking for sliced tomatoes instead of toast saves the most.
