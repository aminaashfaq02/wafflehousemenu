By Editorial Team•Last updated October 28, 2026

What to order at Waffle House for a low-carb, low-sodium, meat-free or allergy-aware meal, with the numbers for each choice. Waffle House does not certify items for any diet, so use these lists to plan and confirm details at the restaurant.

Shared kitchen: every item is cooked on shared grills and waffle irons. “No wheat”, “no milk” and “no egg” below mean those allergens are not listed in the ingredients. Cross-contact is still possible. With a serious allergy or medical diet, check with the restaurant and your doctor or dietitian.

Dietary Guide FAQ

Waffle House does not label items keto, but it is easy to build a low-carb plate: two scrambled eggs with bacon and sliced tomatoes is about 3g of carbs, 22g of protein and 330 calories. Skip the toast, waffle, biscuit, hashbrowns and grits, which carry most of the carbs.
Orders without meat include eggs, cheese and Toddle House omelets without meat, waffles, regular hashbrowns (smothered, covered, diced, peppered or capped), grits, toast, biscuits and the grilled cheese sandwich. Waffle House does not publish whether cooking fats are animal-based, and everything shares one grill.
Among full plates, the Southern Pecan Pie is the lowest in sodium at about 280mg. Country ham (1,720mg a slice) and grilled chicken (930mg) are the saltiest meats.
Regular hashbrowns, scrambled eggs, bacon, sausage, both hams, grilled chicken, steaks and pork chops list no milk. Toast, biscuits, waffles, grits and cheese all list milk, and everything is cooked on a shared grill.
A plain waffle has about 55g of carbohydrates before syrup. Chocolate chips add 16g, peanut butter chips 17g and pecans 3g.
