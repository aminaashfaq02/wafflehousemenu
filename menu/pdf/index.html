Waffle House Menu PDF

Waffle House publishes its nutrition information on its official website, so that is the place to get its current printable version. We do not host Waffle House’s own files. If you want a menu to print, our independent menu guide prints cleanly from any browser.

Official Waffle House nutrition page → Open our printable menu guide

How to print our menu guide

  1. Open our menu guide.
  2. Press Ctrl + P (Cmd + P on a Mac).
  3. Choose US Letter or A4 paper and select “Fit to printable area”.
  4. Turn off background graphics if you want to save ink.

Prices and items change and vary by location, so treat any printout as a guide and check your local restaurant.
