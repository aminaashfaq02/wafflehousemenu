Waffle House Menu PDF

Waffle House publishes its nutrition information on its official website, so that is the place to get its current printable version. We do not host Waffle House’s own files. If you want a menu to print, use the one-page printable menu below, with typical prices and calories for every item.

Official Waffle House nutrition page → Go to the printable menu

How to print the menu

  1. Go to the printable menu below.
  2. Press the Print this menu button, or Ctrl + P (Cmd + P on a Mac).
  3. Choose US Letter or A4 paper and select “Fit to printable area”.
  4. Turn off background graphics if you want to save ink.

Prices and items change and vary by location, so treat any printout as a guide and check your local restaurant.
